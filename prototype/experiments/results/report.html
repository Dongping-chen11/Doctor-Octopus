How to read this

Because most slots have 1–3 eligible people, top-three outcomes may be identical for every policy. First-choice success is a more sensitive ranking diagnostic here. A higher simulated fill rate means more successful top-three selections under the assumptions baked into the simulation. It does not establish better performance in a clinic. Real acceptance labels, prospective testing, and stakeholder review would be needed.

The app's production-like demo is deliberately left on its established rules-based ranker. This is a separate evaluation artifact.
